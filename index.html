What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shortlist of product links you can use to compare listings before ordering through a shopping agent. For each item, record the original seller link, size, color and listed price. Check whether LoveGoBuy accepts the source link and shows the intended variant before paying.

From product link to shipping estimate

  1. Check the source listing

    Open the product entry and locate the original seller link if it is provided. Paste that link into the agent’s order form and confirm the variant, currency and size chart.

  2. Review warehouse photos

    When quality-control (QC) photos are available, compare the color, labels and measurements with your order. Check the seller’s return window before authorizing shipment.

  3. Compare parcel options

    Enter your destination and the packed weight and dimensions. Check whether the available lines bill by actual weight or volumetric weight.

What to include in your landed-cost estimate

Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and international shipping. Include any estimated taxes, duties or handling charges applicable to your destination, and check whether the shipping quote already includes any of them.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the catalog links go?

The product and category links open HipoBuyFind. Use the product page to check the listing details and find the original seller link when one is provided.

Can I order every listed product through LoveGoBuy?

Compatibility depends on the seller, product and agent restrictions. Check whether LoveGoBuy accepts the original seller URL and shows your chosen variant before paying.

Which QC photos should I look for?

Use photos of your selected variant when available. For footwear, look for the sole, size label and side profile. For clothing, compare garment measurements, seams and print position with the listing.

What if a spreadsheet link stops working?

Search the catalog by item ID or product name. If you find a replacement, recheck the seller, size chart, variant and current price before using the new link.
